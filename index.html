About Us

Founded in 2024 at Teknopark Istanbul (Cube Incubation), ROKET is reshaping the world of industry and production with artificial intelligence.

Our ROKET MVP Factory platform allows you to manage all processes of your business from a single platform, eliminating fragmented structures with a digital twin data model.

We enable you to manage all critical areas from project management to product management, from task management to production, from training to human resources, from a single central hub.

By transforming complex data into understandable insights, we reduce production losses, optimize costs, and accelerate your business.

With ROKET MVP Factory, digital transformation is a fast, measurable, and profitable investment.

Your Business's Digital Operating System: ROKET MVP Factory

We consolidate fragmented data and management structures, the biggest challenge for businesses, into a single point. With ROKET MVP Factory Modules, we create your business's digital data twin and optimize all your business processes end-to-end with AI support.

What Problems Do We Solve?

From Fragmentation to Unity:

We bring together all critical areas managed separately—such as projects, tasks, products, production, processes, human resources, and training—into a single platform.

From Rigid Structures to Scalability:

With our modular and AI-powered structure, we instantly adapt to your business's growth pace. We offer a flexible technological infrastructure that shapes according to your needs with a modular structure.

From Complexity to Easy Implementation:

We make AI "accessible" instead of "unreachable." We add a user-friendly AI experience to your business management that integrates end-to-end with all your business processes without requiring technical knowledge.

From Data Loss to Meaningful Insights:

We transform your fragmented, unreadable, and idle data into valuable assets that operate, are analyzed, and enable you to make strategic decisions.

From High Costs to Operational Profitability:

We bring unplanned downtime and productivity losses in production to an operational state with a digital data twin model, enabling you to reduce costs and manage your business profitably with your data.

From Heavy Software to Agile Solutions:

Instead of expensive, inflexible ERP/MRP/Management applications that require months-long installation processes, we establish a quickly deployable, economical ecosystem tailored exactly to your needs.

Core Features

PMI Compliant Enterprise Governance

International project management standards with complete compatibility. An infrastructure covering Integration, Scope, Time, Cost, Quality, Resource, Communication, Risk, Procurement, and Stakeholder management — all under one roof, faithful to methodology.

AI-Powered Decision Support

The platform doesn't just collect data, it analyzes and alerts you. It detects project risks in advance, flags performance anomalies, recommends resource optimization. Executives make decisions with insights, not data.

Role, Permission & Security Management

Create sophisticated access control with unit, role, and user-based permissions. Control who sees what, who can edit what — customize your entire access hierarchy to match your organizational structure.

Universal Integration Infrastructure

Connect to your existing ERP systems, Supply Management Portals, and Active Directory infrastructure via REST Web Services. MVP FACTORY doesn't invalidate your organization's existing technology investments — it strengthens them.

Enterprise Analytics & Reporting

Perform performance analysis at every level from portfolio to task. View the specific sections you need with detailed filters, export reports in different formats, base your executive presentations on data.

Digital Twin Method

Create a digital model of all your organization's processes. Whatever happens in the real world, the platform reflects it instantly. Generate scenarios, run simulations, test your decisions — before bringing them to life.
